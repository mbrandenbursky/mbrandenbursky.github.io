UPLOAD THE COMPLETE LOCAL-ONLY WEBSITE
Michael Brandenbursky | 21 October 2026

Use this ZIP instead of every previous website package.
You must upload ALL its contents, not only index.html.

1. On Windows, right-click the downloaded ZIP and choose Extract All.
   Open the extracted folder. You should see index.html alongside the PDF files.

2. Open your existing GitHub repository:
   github.com/mbrandenbursky/mbrandenbursky.github.io
   Click Code, then Add file, then Upload files.

3. Drag all the files INSIDE the extracted folder onto the upload page.
   Wait until every file has finished uploading.
   Do not upload the ZIP or an enclosing folder.
   index.html must stay at the top level, next to the PDFs.
   Do not rename index.html to index (1).html.

4. Enter "Install complete local-only homepage" as the commit message.
   Choose Commit directly to the main branch, then Commit changes.
   Files with matching names will be replaced and new files will be added.

5. If Pages already publishes from main and /(root), leave the settings as they are.
   Otherwise select Settings > Pages > Deploy from a branch > main > /(root) > Save.

6. Wait for deployment to finish, then open mbrandenbursky.github.io.
   Reload the page. On Windows, Ctrl+F5 can help when the old page is cached.

To preview before uploading, double-click index.html in the extracted folder.
Keep all the files together. Do not delete and recreate your repository.

The page has no remote hyperlinks or resource dependencies. Its paper titles,
arXiv labels and CV link all open included local PDFs. Your university affiliation
and email address have not been removed.
