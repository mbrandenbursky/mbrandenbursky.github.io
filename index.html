About

Associate Professor of Mathematics at Ben-Gurion University of the Negev, Israel.

Email: brandens@bgu.ac.il

Research interests

Symplectic geometry and Hamiltonian dynamics; bounded cohomology and quasimorphisms; geometric group theory; braid and mapping class groups; knot theory and low-dimensional topology; geometry of singularities.
