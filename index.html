Michael Brandenbursky

I am a faculty member at Ben-Gurion University, Israel.

Office: Deichmann 306
Email address: brandens@bgu.ac.il


Research interests

  • Knot Theory: Vassiliev invariants, concordance group, Heegaard Floer and Khovanov homologies
  • Hamiltonian dynamics, symplectic geometry, low-dimensional topology
  • Braid groups, mapping class groups and transformation groups of smooth manifolds: quasi-isometries, quasi-morphisms, cohomology, quasi-norms
  • Geometric group theory: quasi-isometric embeddings of finitely generated groups, bi-invariant word metrics
